Audhesive

Site Name

Sound Stack – the name combines "audio" and "cohesive" to create a catchy, memorable brand that will immediately suggest the site's content. The logo will be positioned on the left side of the header.

Possible domain: audhesive.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to take the target audience, such as aspiring music producers and beginners, through the foundational process of creating a song by sticking individual instrument tracks together one piece at a time to produce a complementary and cohesive beat.

Scenarios

  • How does a track sound when individual instrument layers are muted or played together?
  • Which instrument layer should I start with when making a track from scratch?
  • Can I produce decent music without knowing complicated music theory?
